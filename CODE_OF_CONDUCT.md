# Code of Conduct for SynapsJS
Welcome to SynapsJS, a tiny reactive DOM micro-engine built by Protawn. This project is meant to be simple, helpful, and enjoyable for the developer community. To keep things running smoothly (and pleasantly), here’s a lightweight code of conduct.

Nothing too serious, just the basics of being a decent human while interacting here.

## The Vibe
SynapsJS is a chill, low-maintenance open-source project. Contributions are welcome, curiosity is encouraged, and kindness is appreciated.

Let’s keep the atmosphere:

- Friendly
- Respectful
- Helpful
- Patient
- Not overly dramatic

Basically: treat others like you’d treat someone who just helped you debug a nasty async bug.

## Expected Behavior
Please:

- Be respectful and considerate
- Keep discussions constructive
- Assume good intentions
- Offer help when you can
- Be patient with others (and with me — I’m juggling a lot)
- Keep feedback focused on the code, not the person

If you disagree with someone, that’s fine just do it politely. We’re all here because we like building things.

## Unacceptable Behavior
The usual suspects:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Hate speech or discriminatory language
- Trolling or intentionally derailing conversations
- Spamming issues or PRs
- Being aggressively demanding of time, attention, or labor
- Turning a tiny micro-engine into a battleground of strong opinions

If someone crosses these lines, maintainers (meaning: me, when I have time and coffee) may step in.

## Maintainer Involvement
A gentle reminder:

- My availability is limited.
- I will respond, review, and participate on a best-effort, when-I-can-and-when-I-feel-like-it basis.

Please keep expectations reasonable. I’m here because I enjoy building things, not because I’m running a customer support desk.

## Reporting Issues
If you see behavior that violates this code of conduct:

- You can open an issue
- Or send a message through the appropriate channel
- Or simply disengage and let me know when you can

I’ll address it when I’m able.

## Final Note
SynapsJS is meant to be fun, helpful, and lightweight, both in code and in community expectations. Thanks for keeping things friendly and for helping make this a positive space for developers.

And seriously: thanks for being here.
